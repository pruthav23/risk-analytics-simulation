1) BORROWER EVENTS (from the simulation's scenario questions)
==============================================================================
  New mortgage on top of existing debt [Persona 2]
    Takes a $300,000 mortgage (illustrative amount) without repaying the existing $100,000
    Expected effect: Increase: higher total debt
    Persona 2  score  57.5 ->  44.2   DTI   56% ->  222%   Medium Risk
  Debt consolidation [Persona 3]
    Combines debts into one loan; total debt unchanged
    Expected effect: Decrease: one payment is easier to manage, often at a lower rate
    Persona 3  score  44.0 ->  44.0   DTI   90% ->   90%   Medium Risk
    (scorecard inputs unchanged: the benefit is real but the model cannot see it)
  Liquidate assets to fund a start-up [Persona 1]
    Moves $200,000 of savings (illustrative amount) into a new venture
    Expected effect: Increase: smaller liquid buffer
    Persona 1  score  82.1 ->  72.1   DTI   54% ->   54%   Low Risk
  Unused $10,000 credit card fully drawn [Persona 1]
    Worst case for an undrawn line: the full $10,000 limit becomes debt
    Expected effect: Increase (marginal): an undrawn limit is potential exposure
    Persona 1  score  82.1 ->  81.4   DTI   54% ->   57%   Low Risk
  Three-year salary agreement [Persona 2]
    Income is contractually fixed for three years
    Expected effect: Decrease: income is more certain
    Persona 2  score  57.5 ->  57.5   DTI   56% ->   56%   Medium Risk
    (scorecard inputs unchanged: the benefit is real but the model cannot see it)
  $50,000 business loan repaid on schedule [Persona 4]
    Loan is already part of total debt; two years of on-time repayments
    Expected effect: Decrease: proven repayment history
    Persona 4  score  47.8 ->  47.8   DTI  133% ->  133%   Medium Risk
    (scorecard inputs unchanged: the benefit is real but the model cannot see it)

##### 03_mitigation_matrix.py

==============================================================================
1) MITIGATION MATRIX (strategy chosen for each risk level)
==============================================================================
  An increase in property taxes
    Low     Apply for tax reassessment                           [Low-cost action]
    Medium  Explore legal avenues for tax reduction              [Operating cost]
    High    Increase rental prices moderately                    [Revenue (NOI protection)]
  $250k+ in needed major repairs
    Low     Create a dedicated repair fund                       [Cash reserve]
    Medium  Negotiate extended payment terms with contractors    [Cash-flow timing]
    High    Plan for a one-time rent increase                    [Revenue (NOI protection)]
  10% drop in occupancy rate
    Low     Enhance marketing efforts                            [Low-cost action]
    Medium  Introduce competitive pricing                        [Revenue (NOI protection)]
    High    Upgrade amenities to retain and attract tenants      [Capital investment (CapEx)]
  Market downturn affecting property values
    Low     Adjust rent to market conditions                     [Revenue (NOI protection)]
    Medium  Strengthen tenant relationships through incentives   [Operating cost]
    High    Diversify property portfolio                         [Capital investment (CapEx)]
  New development competition
    Low     Research competitor's pricing and services           [Low-cost action]
    Medium  Introduce loyalty programs for existing tenants      [Operating cost]
    High    Invest in property renovations                       [Capital investment (CapEx)]

==============================================================================
2) HOW THE RESPONSE ESCALATES WITH RISK LEVEL
==============================================================================
  Lever                            Low  Medium    High
  Low-cost action                    3       0       0
  Cash reserve                       1       0       0
  Cash-flow timing                   0       1       0
  Operating cost                     0       3       0
  Revenue (NOI protection)           1       1       2
  Capital investment (CapEx)         0       0       3
  Low risk -> low-cost and reserve actions; medium -> operating and cash-flow
  measures; high -> capital investment (CapEx) or direct revenue moves to protect NOI.
